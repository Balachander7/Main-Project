# Workforce Ecosystem 360: Decoding Sourcing Channel Efficiency, Operational Productivity Ecosystems, Absenteeism Patterns and Predictive Attrition Retention Strategies

An end-to-end People Analytics project analyzing talent acquisition efficiency, operational productivity, attendance patterns, and predictive attrition across a 1,000-record enterprise dataset using Python, Excel, and Power BI.

---

## 📊 Project Overview
This project evaluates the complete employee lifecycle—from recruitment sourcing channels to post-hire productivity and retention risk drivers. It transforms raw workforce data into a 3-page interactive Power BI dashboard suite to support strategic HR decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Python & Google Colab:** Data cleaning, missing value handling, and exploratory analysis (`Balachander V _ Main Project.ipynb`).
* **Excel & Power Query:** Data transformation, formatting, and feature engineering.
* **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling (Star Schema), calculated DAX measures, interactive visualizations, and PDF report generation.

---

---

## 📈 Dashboard Architecture & Insights

1. **Sourcing Efficiency Dashboard:** 
   * Tracks hiring channel volume (Naukri, LinkedIn, Career Site, etc.) and cost-per-hire metrics (average ~₹1,445).
   * Analyzes channel-specific attrition rates and candidate quality.

2. **Operational Productivity & Capacity Ecosystems:** 
   * Measures productivity scores (~59.71 average) and KPI achievement rates across work modes (WFH, Onsite, Hybrid).
   * Highlights the direct positive correlation between training hours and KPI performance.

3. **Predictive Retention, Absenteeism & Exit Analytics:** 
   * Identifies flight-risk indicators (tracking attendance anomalies like late arrivals and unplanned absences).
   * Highlights departmental turnover variations (e.g., Marketing at 34.78% vs. Sales at 25.15%).

---

## 💡 Conclusion
The integration of Python, Excel, and Power BI provides a robust framework for workforce analytics. By bridging sourcing efficiency, skill development, and attendance anomalies, leadership can transition from reactive exit tracking to proactive retention and workforce optimization.
